73 SPECIAL — number trivia, honestly checked (no cipher, no magic)

73 is the 21st prime.
Its mirror, 37, is the 12th prime.
Mirror of 12 is 21 — the product of 7×3.
73 in binary: 1001001 — a palindrome.
Bonus (not in the original bit): 21 in binary is 10101 — also a palindrome.

Base64, for the curious (plain encoding, not a cipher):
73->NzM=  37->Mzc=  21->MjE=  12->MTI=

Just numbers that line up. Ehrlich, keine Magie.
